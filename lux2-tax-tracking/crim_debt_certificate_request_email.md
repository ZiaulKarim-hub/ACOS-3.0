- **Cost estimate:** CRIM Debt Certificate fee is typically $5–$20 per property; attorney / title-agent service fee varies ($100–$500 per property is normal).
- **Act 60 flag for Primary Home:** The individual titling (Wright & Stephanie Thurston, not an SPE) is atypical for this portfolio and suggests a possible Act 60 / Act 22 (Individual Investors) decreto — which can carry a **property-tax exemption on primary residence up to certain caps**. Confirm the exemption status before adding any "delinquent tax" number to the payoff memo — this parcel may legitimately owe $0 if the decreto is active and in compliance.
- **Lien priority reminder:** In PR, unpaid CRIM property taxes are a **first-ranking municipal lien** that primes all subsequent mortgages per Article 6 of the Law of Municipal Property Tax (Law 83-1991). Same superpriority dynamic as US jurisdictions.
